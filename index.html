Full-Day Event Coverage

ठूला तथा विस्तृत कार्यक्रमका लागि full-day professional recording.

03

समाचार / विज्ञापन सामग्री

समाचार तथा promotional/ad content का लागि आवश्यक video production.

04

Promotional Video

व्यवसाय, संस्था, कार्यक्रम वा ब्रान्डका लागि professional promotional content.

05

Social Media Video

Facebook, TikTok, Instagram तथा अन्य social platforms का लागि optimized video content.

06

Event Highlight Video

कार्यक्रमका महत्वपूर्ण क्षणलाई cinematic highlight मा प्रस्तुत गर्ने।

07

Professional Video Editing

Professional editing, pacing, transitions, sound तथा post-production.

08

Raw Footage Delivery

सम्बन्धित पक्षले माग गरेमा raw footage उपलब्ध गराइने।

Drone Videography

आवश्यकता, स्थान, मौसम, availability तथा applicable permissions अनुसार aerial coverage.

Content Production

Media तथा digital platforms का लागि आवश्यक creative content production.
