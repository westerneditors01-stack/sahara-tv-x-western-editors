Frequently Asked Questions

प्रायः सोधिने प्रश्नहरू र तिनका उत्तरहरू। Answers to commonly asked questions.

१. वार्षिक सहकार्यमा के-कस्ता काम समावेश हुन्छन्?1. What is included in the Annual Collaboration? +
वार्षिक सहकार्यमा प्रमुख कार्यक्रमहरूको coverage, आवश्यकता अनुसार Full-Day Shoot, साना कार्यक्रमहरूको editing लगायत सहमत scope अनुसारका media-production कार्यहरू समावेश हुन्छन्। It includes major event coverage, Full-Day shoots as needed, editing for minor events, and agreed media production services.
२. वार्षिक सहकार्यमा Full-Day Shoot कति पटक हुन्छ?2. How many Full-Day Shoots in the Annual Package? +
यसको कुनै fixed संख्या वा frequency हुँदैन। कुन कार्यक्रममा Full-Day Shoot आवश्यक पर्ने हो भन्ने कुरा कार्यक्रमको condition, nature, coverage requirement र आपसी सहमति अनुसार तय गरिनेछ। There is no fixed frequency. Full-Day Shoots are decided based on event nature, condition, requirement, and mutual consent.
३. Raw Footage प्राप्त गर्न सकिन्छ?3. Can we get Raw Footage? +
सम्बन्धित पक्षले माग गरेमा Raw Footage उपलब्ध गराइनेछ। Raw Footage स्वतः deliver गरिने छैन। Raw Footage is provided upon request. It is not delivered automatically.
४. Full-Day Event Package मा Drone coverage हुन्छ?4. Is Drone coverage included in Full-Day Event Package? +
हुँदैन। Full-Day Event Package मा Drone coverage समावेश छैन। No. Drone coverage is excluded from the Full-Day Event package.
५. Custom package बनाउन सकिन्छ?5. Can we build a custom package? +
सकिन्छ। कार्यक्रमको प्रकृति, coverage requirement र scope अनुसार custom package बारे छलफल गर्न सकिन्छ। Yes! We can discuss tailored packages based on your specific requirements and scope.
